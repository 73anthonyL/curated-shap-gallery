Dataset beeswarms

Word beeswarms over the dataset posts each model's notebook explained, with the same SHAP explainer as the curated plots: for every model, one plot for its test split and one for its validation split. The words are those with the largest total |SHAP| over the explained posts (the order SHAP's own beeswarm uses); each dot is one post containing the word, coloured by the post's true label, and (n) is the number of such posts. 07_TWITTER-ROBERTA_FINE-TUNE, 11_MULTI-CHECKPOINT_LOGIT-POOL explained a sample of each split rather than every post, to bound SHAP's cost. Every plot's values reproduce the model's committed word table (largest difference 5.0×10-9). The plots show words and values only, never a post's text.
